Mobile app wireframe · Interactive prototype

Trackademia

A smart note-taking app that spots dates, times, and events as you write and turns them into Google Calendar reminders. Every frame below is a live screen: type, tap, and navigate inside it. Calendar actions are simulated; no real Google account or event is involved.

  1. Write notes naturally
  2. Dates & times detected
  3. Highlighted inline
  4. Review the reminder
  5. Added to Google Calendar
  6. Edit or remove anytime
