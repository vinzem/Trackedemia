# Trackedemia
Trackademia is a Notion-style workspace built around voice; you speak your notes, and it automatically transcribes them into clean, organized pages, blocks, and databases, so you never lose an idea to slow typing.


With Trackademia, students can create their own personalized academic workspace. They can:

Create and organize notes for different subjects and classes. Create to-do lists for assignments, projects, and personal tasks. Set deadlines and reminders so important requirements aren't forgotten. Manage their class schedule and keep track of their academic activities. Organize subjects into separate workspaces, making information easier to find. Upload and manage files such as presentations, documents, reviewers, and other school materials. Track assignment and project progress from pending to completed. Create checklists for projects, exams, and daily tasks. Customize their dashboard based on what they need to see. Search their workspace to quickly find notes, tasks, or documents. Monitor their academic progress and see what still needs to be completed. Receive notifications and reminders for upcoming deadlines and tasks.
